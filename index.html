ABOUT THE STATION

Dirty Laundromatic Radio is an independent online radio station dedicated to curating and broadcasting the finest underground electronic music 24/7. Our carefully crafted playlists feature a diverse selection of Drum & Bass, Jungle, UK Garage, Breakbeat, 140bpm, Deep Dub, and House Music, ensuring there's something for every discerning listener.

Whether you're a seasoned electronic music veteran or new to the scene, Dirty Laundromatic Radio provides a welcoming space to discover new sounds and connect with like-minded music lovers. Check out our broadcast schedule to see when your favorite shows are on air, and don't forget to follow us on social media for the latest updates and exclusive content.

We're currently expanding our broadcasting team! If you're passionate about underground electronic music and have what it takes to bring the beats, we want to hear from you. Join our crew of music enthusiasts and help shape the future of Dirty Laundromatic Radio!
